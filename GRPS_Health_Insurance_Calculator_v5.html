Methodology & notes
• Employee annual costs are the 2027 Employee Annual Costs from the GRPS 2027 rate sheets for full-time (1.0 FTE) employees. The district pays a fixed hard cap by coverage level; the employee pays the rest. Per-pay deductions are the annual cost divided by the group's number of pays (SNE 20, Exempt 26, GRPSPA 19).
• Where the rate sheet shows a $0.00 employee cost, the district hard cap exceeds the annual premium and the difference is deposited into the employee's HSA. This applies to Value HSA 2000 102/103 Single in all three groups ($8,180.35 hard cap − $8,151.96 premium = $28.39) and is shown as a negative amount.
• Benefits are from the BCBSM Benefits-at-a-Glance documents (Value HSA Level 108/109 and Enhanced HSA Level 036/037 effective 1/1/2027; Value 250 092, Value 500 114, Value 1000 104 and Value HSA 2000 102/103 effective 1/1/2025) and the Priority Health HSA 2500 PH01 Schedule of Benefits (effective 1/1/2026).
• The medical slider (Checkups → Major) sets office, online, urgent care and ER visits plus hospital/procedure charges per person; the Rx slider (None → Specialty) sets generic, preferred brand/specialty and non-preferred fills per person. Two-Person is modeled as 2 people and Three or More as 4. Typical charges: primary care $150, specialist $250, online visit $75, urgent care $350, ER $2,000. On the HSA plans, the full charge goes to the deductible, then coinsurance. On Value 250, Value 500 and Value 1000, each visit costs only the plan copay (never more than the charge) and is not subject to the deductible, per the Benefits-at-a-Glance: Value 250 – $20 PCP / $40 specialist / $20 online / $60 urgent care / $150 ER; Value 500 and Value 1000 – $30 PCP / $50 specialist / $30 online / $60 urgent care / $150 ER. Hospital/procedure charges go to the deductible, then coinsurance, on all plans.
• Rx (30-day retail) is modeled at the stated copay per fill on every plan, with no multiplier: Value 250 – $10 / $40 / $80; Value 500 and Value 1000 – $20 / $40 / $80; Value HSA Level – $10 / $40 / $80; Enhanced HSA Level – $10 / $40 / $40; Value HSA 2000 – $20 / $40 / $80; PH01 – $10 / $40 / $80. On the HSA plans, Rx dollars paid also count toward the deductible and out-of-pocket maximum.
• Two-person and family contracts use family deductible, coinsurance and out-of-pocket amounts applied to total household usage. Rx counts toward the out-of-pocket maximum. Preventive care is covered at 100% and is not included.
